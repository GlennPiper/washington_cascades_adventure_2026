Fuel plan

The whole 325-mile loop burns roughly 20 gallons at a 16 mpg baseline, which sounds easy. The number that matters is not the total.

154 miles from Carson (mile 2) to Packwood (mile 156) with no fuel at all.

That single stretch, on Forest Service roads at degraded economy, is the binding constraint on this trip. Top off in Carson without exception.

Stations on and near the route

Where Route mile Role
Carson, WA 2 Mandatory top-off. Last fuel for 154 route miles. Small-town station on Wind River Rd with a store. Verify hours — do not arrive at 9 PM expecting to fuel.
Stevenson, WA off route, ~7 mi W on WA-14 Backup for Carson. More stations and a full grocery store.
Trout Lake, WA off route, ~12 mi SE via WA-141 Optional mid-Day-1 insurance. Single small station, limited hours. A 24-mile round-trip detour. Not in the route GPX.
Packwood, WA 156 First fuel since Carson. Multiple stations on US 12, plus food, a store and cell service.
Randle, WA 217 Last fuel before the final 108 miles. Station and store on US 12; the Cowlitz Valley Ranger Station is here.
Morton, WA off route, ~20 mi W of Randle Backup if Randle is closed. Also the nearest hospital.

The two gaps

Carson (mile 2) to Packwood (mile 156) — 154 miles

Mostly forest road. At a degraded 13 mpg that is about 12 gallons, which most rigs handle on one tank. The ones that should worry:

  • small tanks (under 18 gallons usable)
  • anything with a roof rack, roof tent, or a trailer
  • 35s and up on regeared axles
  • anyone who habitually runs down to a quarter tank

If you are in that group, carry a jerry can. The only bail-out is Trout Lake, and reaching it costs a 24-mile round-trip detour off Day 1.

Randle (mile 217) to the Gorge (mile 325) — 108 miles

Fill at Randle. The last 108 miles cross Elk Pass and the Lewis River country with nothing open. This is a smaller gap but there is no bail-out at all once you are south of Randle.

Forest-road economy runs well below highway numbers. Plan on 65 to 82 percent of your normal mpg depending on surface, and worse if you are airing down and running low range.

Travel days

The drive out and back is 384 and 368 highway miles respectively — about 7.7 hours out (including the FR 68 climb to camp) and 6.8 hours back.

  • Fill in Nampa before departure.
  • One fuel stop somewhere in eastern Oregon (Baker City, La Grande or Pendleton) covers the outbound leg comfortably.
  • Fuel again at Carson (or Hood River / Stevenson) before leaving pavement for Triangle Pass. The Carson top-off is the last pump for 154 route miles.
  • On the return, fuel at Carson or Stevenson before getting on WA-14 eastbound.

Notes

  • Two full refuels on route: Packwood at mile 156 and Randle at mile 217.
  • Carry a funnel if you carry a can. Rotopax and NATO cans both pour badly into modern capless fillers without one.
  • Diesel is available at Packwood and Randle but confirm before relying on it at Carson.
  • Nothing on this route requires premium.
  • No water crossings on this route, so no fording-related fuel-system concerns.
